How It Works

From your portal to your customer's ECU in 4 steps.

  1. 01

    Set Up Your Portal & Upload Tuned Files

    Access your dedicated dealer dashboard. Download certified ORI files and map your Stage 1, Stage 2, or custom calibrations with your chosen credit price.

  2. 02

    Provide Dongles to Your Customers

    Assign your serialized hardware dongles to your clients through your panel and top up their credits.

  3. 03

    Customer Connects & Selects Your Tune

    At home, the vehicle owner plugs the dongle into the OBD-II port and opens your branded app. The system automatically runs a diagnostic and saves a log to your dashboard, reads the ECU ID and VIN, and displays only the matching tunes you provided.

  4. 04

    Safe, Encrypted Flashing

    The file streams securely into the hardware buffer and writes to the ECU. The USD $30 platform fee is charged only when the flash succeeds, and your customer gets a professionally tuned vehicle.

Anti-Brick Protection

Remote flashing without the risk.

Multiple layers of protection make sure a remote flash never leaves your customer stranded.

Pre-Flash Battery Guard

The app measures live OBD-II battery voltage, blocking flashing if voltage drops below safe thresholds (e.g. <12.2V).

Memory Pre-Buffering

The full file is downloaded and verified with SHA256 integrity in RAM before initiating writing to the ECU.

Automated Recovery Mode

If a write is abruptly interrupted (e.g. power loss), the app enters emergency mode to safely write back the original file.

Strict VIN Locking & Return to Stock

Tunes are permanently locked to the vehicle's VIN. Customers can restore the original calibration with Return to Stock. The same USD $30 fee applies to every successful flash, including Return to Stock.

FAQ

Questions tuners ask us.

What is a white-label Personal Flasher?

It is a system you offer under your own tuning brand so customers can reprogram their cars at home. You provide the tunes and the dongle; they use your branded mobile app. You retain control of your files, pricing and customer relationships.

Which vehicles does ECUNEX currently support?

Currently, only Toyota diesel and petrol vehicles equipped with Denso Gen2 or Gen3 ECUs. Models include Hilux, Fortuner, Land Cruiser Prado, HiAce, Innova, Corolla, Etios and Yaris, in compatible versions. Confirm the exact ECU, as fitment varies by engine and market. Other brands and ECU generations are not currently supported.

What if a Toyota Gen3 file is missing from the database?

The customer can use Extraction in the app to read the Toyota Denso Gen3 ECU and send the file to the ECUNEX team for inclusion in the database. Extraction is included at no additional cost; it is not a paid ECU flash. This reading feature is available for Gen3 only.

Can I see my customer's fault codes remotely?

Yes. When your customer connects the vehicle, a diagnostic runs automatically and a log is saved to your dealer dashboard. Every diagnostic the customer runs in the app also creates a dashboard log. Your customer can diagnose the vehicle from home, and you can see their fault codes right away without being next to the car.

Can customers extract or steal my modified binary files?

No. The mobile app never writes the binary file to the device storage. The stream is decrypted only in RAM and transmitted in encrypted chunks directly to the dongle buffer.

How do I charge my customers for my files?

You have complete freedom. You manage your own retail pricing and billing channels (PayPal, Stripe, bank transfer, local gateways). You simply assign internal credits to your customer's dongle through your portal.

What does ECUNEX charge distributors?

USD $25 per dongle, USD $30 for every successful ECU flash, including Return to Stock, and a required annual subscription of USD $500 per distributor. The annual subscription price is negotiable. Gen3 Extraction is included without an additional extraction fee.

Can I require customers to accept my own terms and conditions?

Yes. As a distributor, you can write your own contract and enable the contract option. When enabled, end customers must accept your terms and conditions in the app before they can use the equipment. Enabling this option is your choice.

Does the customer see ECUNEX branding?

No. The system operates as a white-label distributor solution. The mobile app presents your business name, logo, and contact channels.

What happens if a flash gets interrupted midway?

The app includes a built-in recovery routine. It will bypass normal identification and automatically restore the original stock file to bring the ECU back to working condition.
